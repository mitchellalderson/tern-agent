01

Stripe colors

The three stripes are the whole brand. Always stacked in this order, orange on top, yellow in the middle, teal on the bottom. They are decoration and emphasis, not body text.

Stripe Orange#F07A2BTop stripe and the primary accent. Primary buttons, selection edge, focus.
Stripe Yellow#F6B92BMiddle stripe. Highlights, hover, focus glow.
Stripe Teal#2A8C8CBottom stripe only. Replaces red, which read as a flag on dark. Destructive buttons still use red.

Shape

Radius 8 for controls, 12 for cards and bubbles, full-round only for pills. Borders are 1px hairline for rest and 1.5–2px ink for anything interactive or active. No drop shadows; depth comes from paper vs. sand.

06

Rules of the road

Do

  • Keep the background cream and let the stripes be the only loud thing.
  • Use ink for text and for any border that means "you can click this".
  • Stack stripes orange, yellow, teal, top to bottom or left to right.
  • Keep copy plain and short. Sentence case.
  • Leave generous empty space. Simple beats busy.

Don't

  • Set body text in orange or yellow. They fail contrast on cream.
  • Put white text on orange. Use ink.
  • Use gradients, glows, glass, or drop shadows.
  • Reorder, recolor, or space out the stripes.
  • Use pure white or neutral gray anywhere.
